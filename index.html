About us

BAMPRO was built around a simple idea: good painting and decorating solutions should be within everyone’s reach.

We believe there is more to this industry than simply painting walls. It’s about understanding people’s needs, finding the right solutions and making the most of every project, regardless of its size or budget.

Our approach is to keep things practical, honest and straightforward. We want to make it easier for people to access solutions that suit their spaces, their expectations and what they can invest.

As we grow, our vision is to bring together different painting techniques, finishes and ideas, creating a business that continues to evolve and offer more to its customers.

We’re not here just to paint. We’re here to make good spaces and good solutions more accessible.
