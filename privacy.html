Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Bampro Painting & Decoration (“Bampro”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through bampropainting.com.au, why we collect it and how we look after it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you give us in the form: your name, phone number, email address, suburb, the type of project, your project details, and any photos or videos you upload.

When you visit the website, we use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site. It collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived at the site, your general location (city or region), your device and browser type, and actions such as tapping our phone number or sending the form. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. It does not tell us who you are.

When you call or email us, we keep the details you share so we can respond and do the job.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan, carry out and follow up on painting work you engage us for.
  • To issue invoices and keep business records.
  • To improve our website and measure how well our advertising works.

We do not sell your personal information, and we will not add you to a marketing mailing list without your permission.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to, for example:

  • Service providers that run our website, email and analytics: Netlify (website hosting and form handling) and Google (email and Google Analytics). These providers may store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • Trades or suppliers working with us on your job, only with the details they need.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files saved by your browser. We use them only for Google Analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on. The website still works if you do.

How we protect and keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including using secure (HTTPS) connections and password-protected accounts. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, tax records), then delete or de-identify it.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
